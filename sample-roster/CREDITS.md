# The sample roster

The fifteen most recent presidents -- Franklin Roosevelt through to the present --
from the Library of Congress's
[Free to Use and Reuse](https://www.loc.gov/free-to-use/presidential-portraits/) sets.
They are here so the app can be tried, taught with, or demonstrated to a room without
putting a student's face on screen. That matters more here than in most apps: the
promise is that your photos never leave your machine, and demonstrating it with real
students would break that promise in the act of making it.

**First and last names only**, because long names make poor quiz answers. The two
George Bushes keep a middle name, since without one they would be the same person.
The rule is the shortest form that is still unique, so it recomputes itself if the
roster changes -- with the earlier, longer roster it also had to separate John Adams
from John Quincy Adams.

**Mounts are cropped off.** Several of these are photographs of a print on a card, and
the card can carry the sitter's name -- Roosevelt's is signed across his. In a game
about recalling names that is the answer written on the card.

Filenames are `First_Last_Presidents.jpg`; the app reads the name from the filename.
