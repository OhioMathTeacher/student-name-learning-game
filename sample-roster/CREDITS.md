# The sample roster

Forty-five presidential portraits from the Library of Congress's
[Free to Use and Reuse](https://www.loc.gov/free-to-use/presidential-portraits/) sets,
so the app can be tried, taught with, or demonstrated to a room without putting a
student's face on screen. That matters more here than in most apps: the promise is that
your photos never leave your machine, and demonstrating it with real students would
break that promise while making it.

**Names are first and last only.** Long names make poor quiz answers. Two pairs keep a
middle name because dropping it would make them identical -- George H.W. Bush and George
W. Bush, John Adams and John Quincy Adams -- and one surname is two words, Van Buren.
The rule is the shortest form that is still unique.

**Mounts are cropped off.** Many of these are photographs of a print on a card, and the
card often carries the sitter's name, signed or printed by the studio. In a game about
recalling names that is the answer written on the card. Fourteen were trimmed
automatically; Buchanan, Cleveland, Grant, Hayes and Jefferson by hand, because an
engraving on cream paper has no brightness edge for an algorithm to find.

Filenames are `First_Last_Presidents.jpg`; the app reads the name from the filename.
