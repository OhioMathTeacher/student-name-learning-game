# The sample roster

Seventy-six photographs that ship with the app so it can be tried, taught with, or
demonstrated to a room without putting a single student's face on screen. That matters
more here than in most apps: this one's promise is that your photos never leave your
machine, and demonstrating it with real students would break that promise while making it.

Three classes, pickable from the class filter:

| Class | Count | Source |
|---|---|---|
| People | 15 | Wikimedia Commons, public domain only |
| Places | 16 | Wikimedia Commons, public domain only |
| Presidents | 45 | [Library of Congress, Free to Use and Reuse](https://www.loc.gov/free-to-use/presidential-portraits/) |

Every Wikimedia image was checked against Commons licence metadata and kept only if it
came back public domain; anything CC BY or CC BY-SA was dropped, which is why there is no
Taj Mahal and no Stonehenge. The Library of Congress publishes its Free to Use sets for
exactly this purpose.

Filenames carry the name and the class, which is how the app reads them:
`First_Last_Class.jpg`, or `Whole Name__Class.jpg` when a name is not two words.
